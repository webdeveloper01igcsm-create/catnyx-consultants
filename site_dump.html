Get In Touch

Setting up a business in Dubai involves several steps including determining the type of business, choosing a company name, obtaining necessary licenses, drafting legal documents, securing office space, and registering with relevant authorities.

The best location depends on various factors such as your target market, industry, budget, and business objectives. Consulting with a business setup expert can help you assess different areas and choose the most suitable location.

Mainland businesses allow for wider market access but require a local sponsor or service agent, while free zone businesses offer 100% foreign ownership but are restricted to operating within the free zone and limited market access.

Dubai offers various business licenses tailored to different activities, including commercial, industrial, professional, and tourism licenses, among others.

Legal and regulatory requirements include obtaining relevant licenses, adhering to local laws and regulations, fulfilling visa requirements, and complying with tax obligations.
